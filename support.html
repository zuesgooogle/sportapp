01 What the app does 这个 App 做什么

SportApp reads the runs you have recorded in Apple Health and lays them out plainly: a single run, how the weeks and months are actually going, and an AI analysis you can follow up with questions.

SportApp 从 Apple 健康读取你记录的运动，把它们如实摊开给你看：单次跑步、周与月的真实走向，以及可以继续追问的 AI 分析。

The app reads from Apple Health only — it never writes to it or changes anything in it.

App 只从 Apple 健康读取数据，不会向其中写入或修改任何内容。

02 Common questions 常见问题

Where does my data come from, and does it leave my phone?

我的数据从哪里来？会离开手机吗？

Your workouts are read from Apple Health and kept on this device. Nothing is uploaded by the app. Data leaves the device in exactly one case: when you trigger an AI analysis, de-identified workout metrics are sent directly to the model service you chose, using your own API key. Your name, email, Apple ID, device identifiers and your location — including routes — are never sent. The privacy policy sets this out in full.

运动数据从 Apple 健康读取后保存在本机，App 不会上传任何内容。只有一种情况会离开设备：你主动发起 AI 分析时，脱敏后的运动指标会直接发送给你自己选定的模型服务，使用的是你自己申请的 API Key。你的姓名、电子邮箱、Apple ID、设备标识以及位置（包括路线）都不会被发送。完整说明见隐私政策。

Why is AI analysis not working?

AI 分析为什么用不了？

Two things are needed, and they are separate:

需要两件事，而且是分开的：

  • The one-time unlock — the one-tap analysis from a run or a period.
  • 一次性解锁 —— 从跑步详情或统计页发起的一键分析。
  • Your own API key — added under Settings → AI. The app does not ship with a key, so this is what actually pays for the model calls.
  • 你自己的 API Key —— 在「设置 → AI」中填入。App 不内置 Key，模型调用由你自己的账号承担。

If you have bought the unlock but analysis still fails, the key is usually the reason: check that it is saved, that the provider is reachable from your network, and that the account behind it has credit.

如果已经购买解锁但仍然失败，问题通常出在 Key 上：确认它已保存、该服务商在你的网络下可以访问、且对应账号还有余额。

Is the unlock a subscription? Can I get it back on a new phone?

解锁是订阅吗？换手机还能用吗？

It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Nothing renews and there is no recurring charge. On any device signed in to the same Apple ID, open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase.

是一次性买断，不是订阅。不会自动续费，也没有周期性扣款。在任何登录同一 Apple ID 的设备上，打开解锁页面点「恢复购买」即可。

How do I get a refund?

怎么退款？

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through them rather than through the app. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, or open the App Store, tap your account, then Purchase History.

购买由 Apple 处理，因此退款也要通过 Apple，而不是在 App 内操作。请访问 reportaproblem.apple.com，或在 App Store 中打开你的账户，进入「购买记录」。

My Health data looks empty. What should I check?

健康数据是空的，该检查什么？

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → SportApp and confirm that reading is allowed. If you have just granted it, pull down to refresh. Recorded workouts also need to exist in Apple Health — if there are none there, there is nothing for the app to show.

打开「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 健康 → SportApp」，确认读取权限已开启。如果刚刚授权，在列表页下拉刷新一次。另外，Apple 健康里本身需要有运动记录 —— 如果那里是空的，App 也就没有内容可显示。

How do I delete my data?

怎么删除我的数据？

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. Analysis results can also be deleted on their own from Settings, and revoking AI consent in Settings → AI stops any further sending and clears the stored results.

删除 App 即会清除它在本机保存的全部内容。分析结果也可以在设置中单独删除；在「设置 → AI」中撤回同意会立即停止后续发送，并清除已保存的结果。
