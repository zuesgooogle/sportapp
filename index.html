01 What is stored, and where 保存什么、保存在哪

The app keeps the workout data it syncs from HealthKit on this device, for display and analysis. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

App 会把从 HealthKit 同步的运动数据保存在本机，用于展示和分析。App 不会将其上传到任何地方。删除 App 即会删除这些数据。

02 How AI analysis works AI 分析如何工作

When you use AI analysis, de-identified workout facts are sent from your device directly to the model service you selected: the summary metrics, the metric evidence behind each number, and sparse metric series. If you type a question, that text goes too. The request is made with the API key you supplied on your own account with that service — the app holds no account with them, and cannot see or change what they do with the data. It is sent when you trigger it, or, if you turn on automatic analysis, when the app analyses your most recent workout on your behalf.

The services you can choose from are DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com), OpenAI (api.openai.com), Anthropic Claude (api.anthropic.com), Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), Moonshot (api.moonshot.cn), Zhipu (open.bigmodel.cn) and Alibaba Qwen (dashscope.aliyuncs.com). Each is a separate company with its own privacy policy, and each may process or retain what it receives under that policy. The app does not keep a copy of complete requests. It does keep the structured result of an analysis on this device, so the result does not have to be paid for twice, and you can delete it at any time in Settings.

使用 AI 分析时，脱敏后的运动事实会由你的设备直接发送到你选定的模型服务：摘要指标、每个数字背后的指标证据，以及稀疏化指标序列。如果你输入了问题，这段文字也会一并发送。请求使用的是你自己在该服务上申请的 API Key——App 与该服务之间没有账号关系，也无法查看或改变它对数据的处理。传输会在你主动触发时发生；若你开启了自动分析，App 会代你就最近一次运动自动发起一次。

可选的服务商为 DeepSeek（api.deepseek.com）、OpenAI（api.openai.com）、Anthropic Claude（api.anthropic.com）、Google Gemini（generativelanguage.googleapis.com）、Moonshot（api.moonshot.cn）、智谱（open.bigmodel.cn）和阿里千问（dashscope.aliyuncs.com）。它们各自是独立公司，各有自己的隐私政策，并可能依据该政策处理或保留收到的数据。App 不会保留完整请求的副本；但会在本机保留分析的结构化结果，以免重复付费，你可以在设置中随时删除。

03 What is never sent 不会发送什么

Your name, email address, Apple ID, account identifiers, device identifiers and your location — including routes and place names — are never sent. Route coordinates stay on the device; analyses are made from workout metrics alone. Your API key is kept in the Keychain, is shown only in masked form, and is never sent to a service other than the one it belongs to.

你的姓名、电子邮箱、Apple ID、账号标识、设备标识以及你的位置（包括路线和地点名称）都不会被发送。路线坐标只留在本机；分析仅依据运动指标完成。你的 API Key 保存在钥匙串中，仅以掩码形式显示，且只会发送给它所属的那个服务。

04 Changing your mind 撤回同意

You can revoke your consent at any time in Settings → AI. Revoking stops any further sending immediately and deletes the AI results stored on this device. Individual conversations can be deleted too. Revoking does not affect data that a service you chose has already processed.

你可以随时在「设置 → AI」中撤回同意。撤回会立即停止后续发送，并删除本机保存的 AI 结果。单条对话也可以删除。撤回不影响你选择的服务商已经处理过的数据。
